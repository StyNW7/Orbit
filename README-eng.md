## 🧭 Summary

Career preparation today is scattered across many places: job listings on job portals, courses on learning platforms, papers on Google Scholar, notes in Notion, and advice from chatbots that do not know the user. The problem is not a lack of information. **The problem is that nothing connects that information to the user and tells them what to do first.**

**Orbit** brings CVs, skills, experiences, goals, opportunities, learning resources and role models together into one living **Career Universe**. On top of it runs the **Orbit Career Agent**, an AI Agent orchestrated with **IBM Langflow**. The agent reads the user's context, uses 14 tools, and proposes real actions that run only after the user approves them.

```text
Understand me → Map my universe → Discover opportunities → Identify gaps → Build my journey → Take action → Track progress → Adapt ↺
```

**Design principle:** *Bright, not noisy. Dynamic, not chaotic. Gamified, not childish. Intelligent, not complicated.*

### 1. Project Title

> **Orbit: AI Career Navigator for Growth & Job Readiness**

### 2. Project Theme

> **Education & Future of Work**

Orbit helps students and young talent move from education into the world of work. It translates a career target into the skills to learn, the experience to build and the opportunities to take.

### 3. Short Project Description

> Orbit is an AI-powered *Agentic Career & Personal Growth Platform* that helps university students and fresh graduates understand where their career stands today, find their gaps, and know the best next step to become *career-ready*.
>
> The main problem it solves is fragmented career preparation. Job listings live on job portals, courses on learning platforms, papers on Google Scholar, notes in Notion, and advice in generic chatbots. None of them understands the user's context, so students are confused about where to start, which skills they lack, and which opportunities are truly relevant.
>
> Orbit works in one clear flow. The user uploads a CV and picks a target role. Orbit extracts the profile into a *Career DNA* and calculates *Career Readiness* deterministically from six weighted categories. All of this context is mapped into a *Career Universe*, an interactive graph that connects the user with skills, gaps, jobs, events, books, papers and role models. The gaps then become an *Adaptive Career Journey* of milestones, quests, tasks and XP. The *Orbit Career Agent*, orchestrated with IBM Langflow, uses 14 tools to read context, analyze gaps, find opportunities and propose actions, and always asks the user for confirmation before changing data.
>
> The main benefits: a personal, explainable career direction, recommendations that are always tied to the user's goal, measurable progress, and one concrete answer to the most important question, which is what to do next.

### 4. Problem Statement

**Before Orbit.** A student who wants to become a Product Manager usually:

1. Opens **LinkedIn / job portals** to look for openings, then judges the fit on their own.
2. Searches for **courses** on YouTube or learning platforms without knowing which skill is most urgent.
3. Reads **articles or papers** at random, with no link to their goal.
4. Tracks progress in **Notion or a spreadsheet** that gives no feedback.
5. Asks a **generic AI chatbot** that does not know their CV, experience or ongoing applications, so the answers are generic and repetitive.

**Main pain points:**

| Pain point | Impact on the user |
| --- | --- |
| 🧩 **Information scattered** across 5–8 different platforms | Time goes into searching and switching apps instead of growing |
| ❓ **No sense of where they stand** ("How ready am I?") | No objective, explainable measure of readiness |
| 🎯 **No sense of priority** ("Where do I start?") | They learn many random things while the biggest gap stays untouched |
| 🔗 **Recommendations not tied to goals** | Jobs, books and events are recommended because they are popular, not because they are relevant |
| 📉 **Progress is not measured** | Motivation drops because there is no proof of progress |
| 🤖 **AI without context and without action** | Chatbots only return text, do not understand history and cannot act |

**Why it matters.** The move from campus to work is one of the most decisive phases for young Indonesian talent. Without a clear direction, many graduates apply for roles that do not fit, build their portfolio too late, or miss opportunities that actually suit them. Helping them take **the right step, at the right time, for a clear reason** directly improves job readiness and the quality of future talent.

### 5. Target Users

| Segment | Who | Main needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🎓 **Primary: University students (Diploma–Master's)** | Mid- and final-year students preparing for an internship or first job | Know their skill gaps, find suitable internships, build a portfolio |
| 🧑‍💼 **Fresh graduates & job seekers** | Graduates with 0–2 years of experience looking for their first or second job | Prioritized preparation, application tracking, interview practice |
| 🔄 **Career switchers** | Young professionals changing paths (for example Software Engineer → Product Manager) | Compare several career paths and map *transferable skills* |
| 🏫 **Stakeholders (future expansion)** | University career centers, bootcamps and talent programs | Monitor participants' job readiness in aggregate |

Demo persona: **Stanley Wijaya**, a Computer Science student targeting **Product Manager**. He has a technical background and leadership experience, but still has gaps in **Product Analytics**, **SQL** and **professional product experience**.

### 6. Why Is This Solution Needed?

| The current way | With Orbit |
| --- | --- |
| Job portals match **keywords** | **Hybrid matching** (role 25%, skill 25%, experience 20%, preferences 15%, goal 15%) with "why you match", missing skills and the next action |
| AI chatbots have **no long-term memory** | The agent receives a **context package** from the database (target, readiness, gaps, quest, applications, insights) on every answer |
| Chatbots only **return text** | The agent **acts**: adds quests, saves applications, creates reminders, updates the graph, always with confirmation |
| **Fluctuating** readiness scores "from the AI" | **Deterministic Career Readiness** from 6 weighted categories. The AI only explains, it never invents numbers |
| **Random/popular** recommendations | Every recommendation **must connect** to the user's goal, a gap, the journey, an active opportunity or personal context |
| Progress in a **meaningless** checklist | **Evidence-based** quests: XP, readiness and the Career Universe change when a task is done |
| Many separate apps | **One platform** with 5 main navigation areas and an agent available on every page |

In short, Orbit does not replace LinkedIn, Coursera or Google Scholar. **Orbit is the *intelligence layer* on top of all of them**: it understands, connects, prioritizes, guides, tracks and then adapts.

### 7. Key Features

1. **🧬 Career Intelligence (CV → Career DNA → Readiness).** Extracts a CV (PDF or text) into a structured profile: skills, experience, education and achievements. The result is a **Career DNA** (strengths & gaps) and a deterministic, explainable **Career Readiness Score**.
2. **🌌 Career Universe (Interactive Knowledge Graph).** An orbital graph (Sigma.js + Graphology) that connects the user with roles, skills, gaps, experiences, jobs, events, books, papers and role models. Every node can be clicked to see "why this matters", then **Add to Journey**.
3. **🔭 Opportunity Intelligence (Discover).** Four tabs: **Jobs, Growth, Knowledge, People**. Jobs get a match score and an explanation. Events, books (Google Books, Open Library), papers (OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar) and role models are recommended based on the biggest gap. Applications are tracked from *save* to *offer*.
4. **🗺️ Adaptive Career Journey (Gamified Roadmap).** Gaps become **milestone → quest → task → evidence** with XP, levels, streaks, deadlines, several *career orbits* at once, and a **Career Journal** that detects recurring difficulties in the user's notes.
5. **🤖 Orbit Career Agent (IBM Langflow + 14 Tools + Human-in-the-Loop).** One orchestrator agent in Langflow that reads context, picks tools, answers with the structure *Insight · Why it matters · Next best action · Expected impact*, and proposes **action cards** that run only after the user approves them.

### 8. User Flow

```text
User Input → AI Processing (NestJS) → Langflow (Orbit Career Agent) → Output → User Action → System Adapts ↺
                    ▲                              ▲
                    └──────── built & refined together with IBM Bob ────────┘
```

| # | Stage | What happens |
| :---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | **User Input** | The user signs up, picks a target role, uploads a CV, and fills in interests, preferences and personal context (6 onboarding steps) |
| 2 | **AI Processing** | NestJS extracts the CV text, the AI builds a structured profile (validated with Zod), the deterministic engine calculates **Career Readiness**, and the **Career Universe** graph is built |
| 3 | **Career Intelligence** | The user sees their Career DNA: strengths, main gaps, readiness score and *next best action* |
| 4 | **Explore & Plan** | The user explores the Universe, opens a gap node (for example *Product Analytics*), then clicks **Add to Journey**. The journey and quests are created automatically |
| 5 | **Discover** | Orbit shows internships, events, books, papers and role models for the biggest gap. The user clicks **Save & track** on a job, and Orbit detects the deadline and schedules a reminder |
| 6 | **Ask Orbit → Langflow** | The user asks, for example *"What should I focus on this week?"*. NestJS builds a **context package** and sends it to the **Orbit Career Agent** flow in Langflow. The agent calls tools such as `get_user_context` and `get_next_best_action` |
| 7 | **Output** | A structured Markdown answer, chips for the tools used, and **action cards** (for example "Add *Master Product Metrics* to Journey") |
| 8 | **User Action** | The user clicks **Confirm** or **Not now**. Confirmed actions are executed and written to the audit log |
| 9 | **Adapt** | Task done → XP goes up → readiness is recalculated → the Universe and recommendations update → the loop repeats |

### 9. Use of IBM Langflow

IBM Langflow is Orbit's **AI Agent orchestration layer**. All of the agent's reasoning, tool selection and tool calls happen inside a Langflow flow named **Orbit Career Agent**.

#### a. Workflow / flow built

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    CI[💬 Chat Input<br/>context package + message] --> AG
    LM[🧠 Language Model<br/>Groq Llama 3.3 70B · IBM Granite<br/>via watsonx.ai / Ollama] -- Language Model --> AG
    TL[🧰 Orbit Career Tools<br/>custom component · 14 tools] -- Tools --> AG
    AG[🤖 Agent<br/>Orbit system prompt] --> CO[📤 Chat Output<br/>Markdown answer]
    TL <-. "POST /api/agent/tools/{tool}<br/>x-orbit-tool-key + session_id" .-> API[(Orbit API<br/>NestJS)]
```

The flow file is at [`ai/langflow/flows/orbit-agent.json`](ai/langflow/flows/orbit-agent.json) and can be imported through the Langflow UI. The flow is also built **automatically** by [`infra/scripts/langflow-setup.mjs`](infra/scripts/langflow-setup.mjs) (local) and [`hugging-face/orbit/bootstrap.py`](hugging-face/orbit/bootstrap.py) (production). Both scripts take the component templates straight from the running Langflow (`GET /api/v1/all`), so the flow always matches the installed Langflow version.

#### b. Nodes / components used

| Component | Role in the flow |
| --- | --- |
| **Chat Input** | Receives the message from the Orbit API: the `[ORBIT CONTEXT]` package plus the user's question |
| **Agent** | The reasoning core. It holds the **Orbit system prompt** ([`orbit-system-prompt.md`](ai/langflow/prompts/orbit-system-prompt.md)), which requires every recommendation to connect to the user's goal or gap, forbids inventing data, and asks for confirmation before any change |
| **Language Model** | A model that can be swapped with one variable, `LANGFLOW_MODEL_PROVIDER`: **IBM Granite** on **IBM watsonx.ai**, **Ollama** (local Granite 3.3), or **Groq** (Llama 3.3 70B, used in production because it is fast) |
| **Orbit Career Tools** *(custom component)* | Our own Python component ([`orbit_career_tools.py`](ai/langflow/custom-components/orbit/orbit_career_tools.py)). It reads `GET /api/agent/tools/manifest` and exposes **14 tools** as LangChain `StructuredTool`s |
| **Chat Output** | Returns the agent's final answer to the Orbit API |

#### c. Input & output

| | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Input** | `input_value` = `[ORBIT CONTEXT]` (target role, readiness, top gaps, active quest, applications, recent insights, page context, language) + the user's message. `session_id` = the Orbit conversation ID (short-term memory and identity for tool calls) |
| **Output** | A structured Markdown answer (*Insight · Why it matters · Next best action · Expected impact*), the list of tools called, and **AgentActions** (confirmation cards) created by SUGGEST/WRITE tools |

#### d. AI Agent process

```text
OBSERVE → UNDERSTAND → RETRIEVE CONTEXT → DECIDE → SELECT TOOL → ACT → (USER CONFIRMS) → UPDATE SYSTEM → EVALUATE → RECOMMEND NEXT ACTION
```

1. **Observe & understand.** The agent reads the context package and the user's question.
2. **Decide & select tool.** The agent picks the tools it needs, for example `analyze_career_gap` → `find_opportunities` → `search_books`.
3. **Act.** Orbit Career Tools calls the Orbit API. **READ** tools return data right away. **SUGGEST/WRITE** tools only *propose* an `AgentAction`.
4. **Respond.** The agent writes an answer that explains the reasoning and the impact.
5. **Human-in-the-loop.** The user confirms the action card in the UI, then the `ActionExecutor` applies the change. The graph, readiness and progress update, and everything is written to the audit log.

#### e. Langflow's role in the overall system

- **Orchestration brain.** Langflow decides *when* to use a tool and *which* one. Business logic stays safely in the API.
- **Model-agnostic.** The model can switch between IBM watsonx.ai, Ollama or Groq without changing application code.
- **Secure.** The browser never talks to Langflow directly. NestJS is the *AI gateway*. Tool calls use a separate *shared secret* (`x-orbit-tool-key`, compared in a timing-safe way) and never carry user tokens. Model API keys are stored as Langflow *credential variables*, not inside the flow JSON.
- **Reliable.** Langflow is the primary engine, but not a *single point of failure*. The fallback chain is **Langflow → direct model tool loop → deterministic planner**. After a failed or timed-out Langflow run, Orbit skips it for 5 minutes so users never wait.

### 11. How IBM Langflow and IBM Bob Are Integrated

The two form one cycle: **IBM Bob builds and refines what Langflow runs, and Langflow's execution results feed back into Bob for the next iteration.**

**1. What does Langflow do?**
Langflow runs the **Orbit Career Agent** at runtime. It receives the context package and the user's message, reasons with the language model, calls the 14 Orbit tools, and produces an answer and proposed actions.

**2. What does Bob do?**
Bob builds every "bridge" that lets the Langflow agent work with real Orbit data:
- The **custom component** `Orbit Career Tools` (Python), which Langflow loads as a tool provider.
- The **Tool API** in NestJS (`/api/agent/tools/manifest` and `/api/agent/tools/{tool}`) with its parameter schemas and security levels.
- The agent's **system prompt**, placed in the Agent component.
- The **flow-building scripts** (`langflow-setup.mjs`, `bootstrap.py`) that assemble *Chat Input → Agent → Chat Output* + model + tools through the Langflow API and export `orbit-agent.json`.
- The NestJS **LangflowClient**, the context builder and the fallback chain.

**3. How does data/information move between them?**

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    participant Dev as 👨‍💻 Developer
    participant Bob as 🛠️ IBM Bob
    participant Repo as 📦 Orbit Repo
    participant LF as 🧠 IBM Langflow
    participant API as ⚙️ Orbit API (NestJS)
    participant User as 👤 User

    Dev->>Bob: Agent feature need (e.g. "the agent must be able to save an application")
    Bob->>Repo: Writes tool spec, endpoint, custom component, prompt, flow script
    Repo->>LF: Setup/bootstrap script assembles the flow + loads Orbit Career Tools
    User->>API: "What should I focus on this week?"
    API->>LF: POST /api/v1/run/{flow} (context package + message + session_id)
    LF->>API: Tool calls → /api/agent/tools/* (x-orbit-tool-key)
    API-->>LF: Career data (gaps, opportunities, next best action)
    LF-->>API: Structured answer + proposed actions
    API-->>User: Answer + confirmation cards
    LF-->>Dev: Run traces, errors and answer quality
    Dev->>Bob: Feedback from Langflow results
    Bob->>Repo: Refines prompt, tool schemas and error handling
```

- **Bob → Langflow (build time):** component code, prompt, flow definition and tool schemas flow from the repository built with Bob into Langflow, through the setup scripts or the Hugging Face Space Docker image.
- **Langflow → API → Langflow (runtime):** when a user asks something, Langflow calls the tool endpoints built with Bob, and career data flows back to the agent.
- **Langflow → Bob (iteration):** execution traces, errors and answer quality from Langflow go back to Bob to improve the prompt, tool schemas and fallback logic.

**4. What is the final output of the integration?**
A **fully working AI Career Agent that can act**. It answers from the user's real data in the format *Insight · Why it matters · Next best action · Expected impact*, shows which tools it used, and proposes **confirmable actions** (add a quest, save an application, create a reminder, update the graph). The end result is a user who knows exactly what to do next, and every step they take immediately updates their Journey, Readiness and Career Universe.

### 17. What Makes This Project Different?

1. **🌌 A Career Universe, not a list.** Orbit models a career as a **personal knowledge graph** that connects the user, roles, skills, gaps, opportunities, knowledge and people. The graph is actionable: every node can become a quest in the Journey right away, and the graph changes as the user grows.
2. **⚖️ Explainable hybrid intelligence.** Important numbers (readiness, match score) are calculated **deterministically** with transparent weights. The LLM only **explains and reasons**; it never invents scores, job data or deadlines. The result is consistent, auditable and trustworthy.
3. **🤖 An agent that acts with permission.** The Orbit Agent is not a chatbot. It has long-term memory (a context package from the database), **14 tools**, page-context awareness, and a **human-in-the-loop** flow: it reads automatically, proposes actions, and executes them after confirmation, with a full audit log. It still answers when the model is slow thanks to a 3-layer fallback chain.

### 18. AI Agent Capabilities

The Orbit Career Agent is **one orchestrator agent** in IBM Langflow with **14 tools** split into three security levels:

| Level | Behavior | Tools |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🟢 **READ** (automatic) | Runs right away to fetch data | `get_user_context`, `analyze_career_profile`, `analyze_career_gap`, `find_opportunities`, `search_growth_resources`, `search_books`, `search_papers`, `find_role_models`, `get_next_best_action` |
| 🟡 **SUGGEST** (semi-automatic) | Creates suggestion cards that can be accepted or dismissed | `update_career_graph`, `create_reminder` |
| 🟠 **WRITE** (semi-automatic, confirmation required) | Proposes changes that only run after the user clicks **Confirm** | `build_career_journey`, `update_progress`, `manage_application` |

**Automatic tasks:**
- 📥 Reads and summarizes the user's career context (target, readiness, gaps, quest, applications, insights).
- 🔍 Analyzes skill gaps against the target role (current level vs required level).
- 💼 Finds and ranks jobs with match scores and explanations.
- 📚 Finds courses, events, books, papers and role models for a specific gap.
- 🧭 Determines the *next best action* with its reason and impact.
- 📝 Detects patterns in the Career Journal (for example "Product Metrics mentioned as a difficulty 3 times").
- 🔔 Creates scheduled notifications for deadlines, events, follow-ups and weekly reviews (backend scheduler).

**Semi-automatic tasks (with confirmation):**
- ➕ Creates or expands the Career Journey (milestones and quests) from gaps.
- ✅ Marks a quest task as done when the user says they finished it.
- 💾 Saves a job as an application or changes an application's status.
- ⏰ Creates reminders from natural language ("tomorrow 19:00", "in 2 days", "next monday").
- 🌌 Adds new nodes to the Career Universe.

**Example interaction:**

```text
👤  "I want to become a Product Manager. What should I focus on this week?"

🤖  get_user_context → analyze_career_gap → get_next_best_action

    INSIGHT           This week's focus: Gain Practical Product Experience.
    WHY IT MATTERS    Product Analytics is your biggest gap. Your APM application at Kopiloka
                      closes in 13 days. Interview Readiness is your lowest category (65).
    NEXT BEST ACTION  Tailor your CV for product roles.
    EXPECTED IMPACT   +5 Stakeholder Management and progress on the active quest.

    [ Open quest ]  [ Add "Master Product Metrics" to Journey ]  [ Not now ]
```

The agent is also **context-aware per page** (Universe, Journey, Discover and more), answers in the **user's chosen language** (ID/EN), and stores every action in the **audit log** under Settings.

## 🏗️ System Architecture

```mermaid
flowchart TB
    subgraph Browser["🌐 Browser: React 19 + Vite (Vercel)"]
        UI[Landing · Onboarding · Mission Control · Universe<br/>Journey · Discover · Progress · Settings · Guide]
        DR[Agent Drawer: Ask Orbit]
    end

    subgraph API["⚙️ NestJS 11 API: AI Gateway (Vercel)"]
        G[AuthGuard · Throttler · ValidationPipe · Helmet]
        M[Domain modules<br/>profiles · readiness · graph · journeys · opportunities<br/>applications · notes · notifications · progress]
        AG[Agent module<br/>Context Builder · Tool Registry · Langflow Client<br/>Direct Loop · Planner · Action Executor · Audit]
        S[Scheduler: cron notifications]
    end

    subgraph Data["🗄️ Data"]
        PG[(PostgreSQL 16 + pgvector<br/>Supabase · 36 Prisma models)]
        ST[(Supabase Storage<br/>CV & evidence)]
    end

    subgraph AI["🧠 AI Layer"]
        LF[IBM Langflow 1.12<br/>Orbit Career Agent<br/>Hugging Face Space]
        LLM[Language Model<br/>Groq Llama 3.3 70B · IBM watsonx.ai Granite · Ollama Granite]
    end

    EXT[🌍 External providers<br/>Remotive · Arbeitnow · WWR · Google Books<br/>Open Library · OpenAlex · Semantic Scholar]

    UI -- "REST /api (Bearer JWT)" --> G
    DR -- "/agent/chat" --> G
    G --> M & AG
    M --> PG
    M --> ST
    M --> EXT
    AG -- "context package + message" --> LF
    LF --> LLM
    LF -- "Orbit Career Tools<br/>/agent/tools/* (x-orbit-tool-key)" --> AG
    AG -. "fallback" .-> LLM
    S --> PG
```

**Key architecture decisions:**

- 🔒 **The browser never calls Langflow or a model directly.** NestJS is the *AI gateway* for security, context injection, validation, logging, rate limiting and tool access control.
- 🧠 **Two kinds of memory.** Short-term memory lives in the Langflow session (`session_id` = conversation ID). Long-term memory lives in PostgreSQL (profile, skills, journeys, quests, notes, applications, insights), not in chat history.
- 🛡️ **Agent fallback chain:** `Langflow → direct model tool loop → deterministic planner`. The agent **always answers**, and Orbit skips Langflow automatically when its model is measured as too slow or has just failed.
- 🧾 **Human-in-the-loop and audit.** READ tools run automatically. SUGGEST/WRITE tools create `AgentAction`s that must be confirmed, and every action is logged.
- 🔐 **Security.** JWTs are verified on every private route and every query is filtered by user ID. There are whitelisted DTOs, Zod for AI output, magic-byte upload validation, Helmet, restricted CORS, a stricter rate limit on `/agent/chat`, and an *error envelope* without stack traces. Secrets live only on the server; the browser sees only `VITE_*` values.

More detail in [`docs/architecture/README.md`](docs/architecture/README.md) and [`ai/langflow/README.md`](ai/langflow/README.md).

## 🧮 Deterministic Engines

> **"The AI explains, the engine calculates."** Numbers that drive the user's decisions must not fluctuate, so Orbit calculates them deterministically.

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Career Readiness Score** (configurable through `READINESS_WEIGHTS`)

| Category | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Skill Match | **30%** |
| Experience Match | **25%** |
| Portfolio | **15%** |
| Knowledge | **10%** |
| Application Readiness | **10%** |
| Interview Readiness | **10%** |

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Hybrid Job Matching**

| Component | Weight |
| --- | ---: |
| Role Alignment | **25%** |
| Skill Match | **25%** |
| Experience Match | **20%** |
| Preference Match | **15%** |
| Career Goal Match | **15%** |

</td>
</tr>
</table>

Recommendation pipeline: **structured filtering → vector similarity (pgvector, 768-dimension embeddings) → deterministic ranking → LLM reasoning**. Every match returns per-component scores, matched and missing skills, reasons, and a suggested action.

## 🚀 Running Locally

### Prerequisites

- **Node.js 20+** and **pnpm 9** (`corepack enable`)
- **Docker Desktop** (PostgreSQL, Ollama, Langflow)
- Optional: a Supabase project, a Groq API key, or IBM watsonx.ai credentials

### Quick start

```bash
pnpm install
cp .env.example .env                 # then review the values

pnpm infra:up                        # postgres + ollama + langflow (docker)
pnpm ollama:pull                     # granite3.3:2b + nomic-embed-text (first time only)

pnpm db:generate
pnpm db:migrate                      # applies the Prisma migrations (enables pgvector)
pnpm db:seed                         # roles, skills, jobs, books, papers, events, role models, demo user

pnpm langflow:setup -- --write-env   # builds the Orbit Agent flow in Langflow and stores LANGFLOW_FLOW_ID

pnpm dev                             # web http://localhost:5173 · API http://localhost:4000/api
```

Open http://localhost:5173, then sign up or click **Explore the demo account** (`demo@orbit.app` / `OrbitDemo2026!`).

### Choosing the Langflow model

| `LANGFLOW_MODEL_PROVIDER` | Needs | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ollama` (default) | `AI_MODEL` pulled | Fully local (IBM Granite 3.3). Too slow for live answers on machines without a GPU, so Orbit automatically uses another engine and explains why in Settings |
| `groq` | `GROQ_API_KEY` | Fast, with a free tier. Used in production (Llama 3.3 70B) |
| `watsonx` | `WATSONX_API_KEY`, `WATSONX_PROJECT_ID` | IBM Granite on **IBM watsonx.ai** |

```bash
LANGFLOW_MODEL_PROVIDER=watsonx pnpm langflow:setup -- --write-env
```

<details>
<summary><b>🩺 Troubleshooting</b></summary>

<br/>

| Symptom | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| API cannot reach the database | Run `pnpm infra:up`, then check that `DATABASE_URL` points at port `54322` |
| `vector` type missing | Use the `pgvector/pgvector` image (compose default) or enable the extension in Supabase |
| Agent shows "Orbit planner" | Expected without a fast model. See **Settings → Orbit Career Agent**, then configure Groq or watsonx |
| Langflow setup: "Orbit Career Tools not available" | `docker compose -f infra/docker-compose.yml up -d langflow` (the custom component folder must be mounted) |
| Langflow "SSRF Protection" error | `LANGFLOW_SSRF_ALLOWED_HOSTS` must include `ollama` (already set in compose) |
| Flow disappeared after recreating Langflow | Re-run `pnpm langflow:setup -- --write-env` |
| Demo login fails after re-seeding | The seed recreates the demo user. Sign in again |

</details>

### Golden demo flow (~3 minutes)

1. **Sign up**, pick **Product Manager**, then use the sample CV.
2. **Career Intelligence** appears: readiness, strengths, main gaps and next best action.
3. **Universe**: search "Product Analytics", open the gap node, then click **Add to Journey**.
4. **Journey**: complete a task. Readiness and XP go up, and the Universe changes with them.
5. **Discover**: *Top picks* shows an internship, an event, a book, a paper and a role model for the biggest gap. Click **Save & track** on a job, and Orbit detects the deadline and schedules a reminder.
6. **Ask Orbit**: *"What should I focus on this week?"* The answer uses your journey, gaps, application deadline and progress, with action cards.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    U([👤 User]) --> W[Vercel<br/>orbit-career-agent.vercel.app<br/>React + Vite PWA]
    W --> A[Vercel Serverless<br/>orbit-api-agent.vercel.app/api<br/>NestJS]

    A --> HF[Hugging Face Docker Space<br/>Langflow 1.12.5<br/>Orbit Career Agent]

    HF -- tool calls --> A
```

| Component | Platform | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Web | Vercel | Static Vite build + PWA |
| API | Vercel (serverless) | NestJS, health check at `/api/health` |
| Database, Auth, Storage | Supabase | Postgres with the `vector` extension, private `orbit-files` bucket |
| Langflow | Hugging Face Docker Space | `hugging-face/Dockerfile` (base `langflowai/langflow:1.12.5`). `bootstrap.py` rebuilds the flow under a **fixed ID** on every boot, so `LANGFLOW_FLOW_ID` never changes |
